Haruspex: Reign of the Source
A RimWorld storyteller mod built for consequence, ritual, and narrative emergence.
Welcome to the Source. This mod reimagines the RimWorld experience through the eyes of the Haruspex, a truth-seeking, reckoning-driven force that reshapes gameplay around your choices, failures, and legacies.

🔮 Features
- Storyteller logic: Custom storyteller rewrites event pacing based on moral drift and relic possession
- Ritual mechanics: Player-initiated rituals with gameplay effects, mood shifts, and potential backlash
- Legendary gear: Biocoded items and implants tied to ancient lore and late-game crafting
- Narrative consequences: Events escalate based on philosophy, risk, and inherited decisions
- Modular systems: Fully extensible folder structure (Defs, XML, C#) for easy updates and scalability

Philosophy
This isn’t just a mod—it’s a reckoning. Haruspex challenges the player to lead, lose, and reflect. Every item, ritual, and event branches meaningfully. Victory is survival with scars. Legacy is what survives you.
